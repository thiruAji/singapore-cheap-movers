    How much do movers cost in Singapore?

    Our base rates are $180 (10ft lorry), $280 (14ft tailgate lorry) and $480 (24ft jumbo lorry). Each includes the lorry, driver, movers, fuel and stretch-film wrapping for lift-landing moves. Dismantling, boxes, stairs and disposal are itemised add-ons.

    Is dismantling and assembly included?

    It's charged separately so you only pay for what you need: from $30 for a standard bed or dining table, from $60 for storage or bunk beds, and from $80 for wardrobes. You'll see the exact amount on your quote.

    How many lorries do I need for a 4-room HDB?

    Most 4–5 room flats fit in one 24ft jumbo lorry or two 14ft lorries. 2–3 room flats usually fit in one 14ft lorry. Send us a quick video and we'll tell you exactly.

    Do you handle condo MCST moving permits?

    Yes. After you book, we send our vehicle numbers, crew details and insurance certificate so you can submit the move-in or move-out form to your condo management.

    How early should I book?

    1–2 weeks ahead for weekends, month-end and auspicious dates. We also take same-day and next-day moves when lorries are available.

    What if something gets damaged?

    All moves include goods-in-transit insurance. Check your items when we unload, and report any issue to your crew leader before they leave so we can sort it out quickly.
