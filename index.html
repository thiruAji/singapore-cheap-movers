    What are your truck moving rates in Singapore?

    1× 10ft Truck (1 Driver + 1 Helper): $200 per load.
    1× 14ft Truck (with 2 Helpers): $350 per load.
    Both packages include protective wrapping, 30 free boxes (return collection), and basic dismantling & assembly.

    What dismantling & assembly is free vs add-on?

    Included FREE: Basic TV wall-mount dismantling, sofa legs, basic dining table dismantling/assembly, and standard bedframes.
    Add-on ($25–$40): Storage/hydraulic beds, removing & reinstalling fridge doors, and IKEA/sliding wardrobes are $25 to $40 per item.

    How do the 30 free boxes (return collection) work?

    We provide up to 30 carton boxes free of charge for your move. Once you finish unpacking at your new home, just message us at 8407 6882 and our team will come collect the empty boxes back.

    Do you provide furniture storage services?

    Yes! As Dheeran Movers & Storage, we offer safe short-term and long-term storage if your new home is still under renovation or between leases. WhatsApp us your item list for an affordable monthly or weekly storage rate.

    Do you handle condo MCST moving permits?

    Yes. After you book, we send our vehicle numbers and mover details so you can submit the move-in or move-out form to your condo management office.
